Become a Volunteer

Volunteers are the heart of FindAPet. Whether you have a few hours each week or just want to help during special events, your time makes a difference.

Ways You Can Help

  • Walk and Exercise dogs.

  • Socialize cats and small animals.

  • Asset during adoption events.

  • Help clean and organize the shelter.

  • Foster pets while they wait for adoption.

  • Donate pet food, blankets, or toys.

    • Why Volunteer?

    Volunteering is a rewarding experience that helps animals feel safe, healthy, and loved while they wait for permanent homes. You'll also meet fellow animal lovers and make a positive impact in your community.
